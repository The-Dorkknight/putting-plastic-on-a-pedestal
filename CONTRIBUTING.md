# Contributing

Corrections, prior art I missed, and above all **print results** are very welcome.

- **Printed one of these?** Open an issue with photos and the numbers from section 11 of the paper (what you printed, printer, material, whether the roof stayed flat, the weight of the removed support).
- **Know of earlier work** that describes the same methods? Open an issue with a link. I'll add it to section 8, even (especially) if it makes my "nothing found" look silly.
- **Spotted a mistake?** Issues or pull requests both fine.

Contributions to the text and figures are under CC BY-SA 4.0, and to the code in `tools/` under PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0, the same as the rest of this repository.
